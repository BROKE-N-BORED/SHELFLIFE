How it works

  1. You pick one photo of the item.
  2. ShelfLife makes a magazine style photo of it and suggests a price.
  3. It lists the item on the best sites for 30 days.
  4. You only hear about it if it sells, or when the 30 days are up.
  5. Anything unsold goes on your Plato's Closet list.

How to use ShelfLife

Sell something

  1. Tap Sell something on the home screen.
  2. Pick one photo of the item. ShelfLife only sees the photos you pick.
  3. Say what it is and what shape it is in.
  4. Pick a magazine style scene for the photo.
  5. Check the suggested price. Take it or set your own.
  6. ShelfLife ticks the best sites. Untick any you do not want.
  7. Tap List it. Done. Your item gets a serial number like SL-0001.

The 30 days

Every listing runs for 30 days. You will not hear from ShelfLife during that time unless the item sells. On day 30 the listing comes down and the item moves to your Plato's Closet list.

The price guide

ShelfLife suggests a price from a built-in guide for the item type and condition. It is a guide, not live sales data. Tap Check live prices to see what the same item really sold for, then set your price.

Your photos

ShelfLife never sees your whole photo library. You pick one photo at a time with your phone's own picker, and that is the only photo the app ever gets.

Your account

Your email and password are saved only on this device. ShelfLife never sends them anywhere. Your items, photos, and lists live on this device too.

An honest note

This copy of ShelfLife manages your listings inside itself: it tracks them, counts the 30 days, and tells you when something sells or time is up. It does not post straight to the real marketplaces. To put a listing live on a real site, open the site from the listing and post it there. Your serial number, photos, and details stay with the item here.
